About the study

What is this research about?

This study looks at how ordinary people, acting as a juror would, reason about a fictional forensic case. You will read a short scenario and discuss your judgements about the responsibility and risk involved. There are no right or wrong answers, and the case is entirely fictional. Your views help shape how forensic science understands lay decision-making.

⚖️

The focus

Evaluating responsibility and risk in a fictional forensic case.

🕐

The format

One 45–60 minute interview, on Microsoft Teams or at Salford campus.

📋

What you'll do

Read and discuss a fictional case. No questions about your personal life.

🎙️

Recording

The interview is audio-recorded so responses can be analysed accurately.

What to expect

Here is exactly how taking part works, from start to finish.

  1. Complete the form belowIt takes around two minutes. Enter your details, choose how you would like to take part, and tick the confirmation boxes.
